Whimsical Text Transformations

Pick your poison. Enter some text. Prepare to giggle.

Oodle

Hello Hoodlelloodle

Wibble

Hello Hibeliblibo

Snorfle

Adds "snorf" to each word

Hello Hellosnorf

Bloop

Adds "oop" after vowels
